# Knowledge base

Reviewed source metadata and rule authoring fixtures for the traditional-practice and Vastu guidance.

## Status: empty, and that is the correct state

Nothing here yet because nothing has been reviewed yet. This directory is the product's actual defensible asset and it is also its hardest launch dependency.

The PRD is unambiguous about how content gets here (sections 13, 22, 23 and 40):

- A rule may not be generated by a language model. It comes from a primary or trusted reference, is reviewed by a person, and is then stored as a structured rule.
- Every rule carries its tradition, region scope, source with edition or section, required inputs, exceptions, reviewer, and lifecycle status.
- A tradition-specific rule must never be presented as a universal Hindu rule.
- The placeholder `verified_reference` that appears in the PRD's illustrative examples is a schema placeholder. It must never ship as a source.

## What has to happen before any traditional finding can be shown

| Task | What it needs |
|---|---|
| P0-06 | Choose the initial supported traditions and topics; name a reviewer and who may publish |
| P6-01 | Collect actual references with title, edition or section or URL, tradition, and attribution |
| P6-02 | Replace every illustrative placeholder with reviewed launch rules |
| P6-03 | Draft, reviewed, published, retired lifecycle with an audit trail |

Until an approved rule exists, the correct behaviour is to return the supported visual observations and state plainly that traditional guidance is unavailable. Architecture section 7 requires exactly that, and the rules evaluator must answer `not_assessed` rather than presuming a pass.

An empty directory here is honest. A directory full of unreviewed rules would not be.
